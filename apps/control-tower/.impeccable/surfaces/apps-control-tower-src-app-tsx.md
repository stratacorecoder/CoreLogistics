# Control tower

Mode: Operate

Audience: control tower planners at a desk. Task: see the live site and move trips, fleet, the map, and analytics. Constraint: real API data and existing actions stay; warehouse, gate, and driver do not inherit these layouts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A frosted operations desk on an ambient mesh. The tower refuses the flat solid-panel console and the left-stripe KPI card.

OWN-WORLD: Three glass levels (default, strong, subtle) over a blue, purple, teal, and green mesh. Apple hues for status. Shells at 20–24px radius. Inter and the system face for UI; JetBrains Mono for ids, plates, and counts.

STORY: The planner signs in, lands on the live site, and can dispatch, check fleet, edit the map, or read analytics without the material changing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating glass sidebar, glass header (site, operator, live, clock, theme). Live is a KPI grid and on-site list, the spatial map with a lifting cell and inspector, then trips and the activity feed.

FORM: Pinned by the user from the liquid-glass mock. No concept roll, so there is no seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
